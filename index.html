What is Hack and Hunt?

A premier inter-college coding competition under VIGYANTRA '26 at SJBIT, Bengaluru. One day. Real problems. One champion team.

💡

What You'll Build

Teams design and develop working solutions to real-world problem statements revealed on competition day. Creativity and speed are both rewarded.

🎯

What You'll Learn

Rapid prototyping, team collaboration, time management under pressure, and pitching ideas to an expert panel — real skills, real exposure.

🤝

Teamwork First

Hack and Hunt is built on collaboration. From developer to presenter — every role matters. The best teams win together, not alone.

⚡

High-Intensity Format

Time-boxed rounds, live judging, and surprise constraints across 04 intense rounds. Only the most adaptable teams rise to the top.

🌐

Open to All Domains

Web dev, AI/ML, cybersecurity, hardware, or business strategy — problem statements span multiple tracks so every talent fits.

🎖️

Recognition & Rewards

Cash prizes, trophies, and certificates for winners. Every participant receives an official participation certificate from SJBIT / VIGYANTRA.

General Rules

Fair play is non-negotiable. Every team must adhere strictly to all official event regulations.

01
TEAM COMPOSITION
Each team must consist of exactly 4 registered members. Team members must remain unchanged throughout the event. Substitution or replacement is not permitted unless approved by the organising committee.
02
TEAM ID & IDENTIFICATION
Each team will receive a unique Team ID at registration. The assigned Team ID must be used throughout the event for identification, submissions, and official records.
03
REGISTERED EMAIL ID
The email ID provided during registration must be used throughout the event whenever an email ID is required, including submissions, forms, communication, and event-related activities. Alternate email IDs may not be used unless authorised by the organising committee.
04
REPORTING & CONDUCT
Teams must report at the scheduled time for every round and follow the instructions of organisers, volunteers, Station Masters, and judges. Participants are expected to maintain professional and respectful conduct throughout the event.
05
PERMITTED RESOURCES
Only the materials, devices, websites, software, and resources specifically permitted for each round may be used. Mobile phones and unauthorised electronic resources are prohibited wherever specified by the round rules. Official clues, props, answer sheets, and other event materials must be used only as instructed.
06
FAIR PLAY
Sharing answers, clues, or solutions with other teams, receiving unauthorised assistance, observing another team's progress, communicating beyond permitted formats, or tampering with event materials is strictly prohibited. Any attempt to gain an unfair advantage may result in penalty or disqualification.
07
TIME LIMITS & SUBMISSIONS
Each round has a fixed time limit. All submissions must be completed within the specified time. Late submissions will not be considered unless otherwise announced by the organising committee. Official submission and completion times may be used for scoring or tie-breaking.
08
ROUND PROGRESSION & SCORING
Hack & Hunt follows a sequential elimination format. Teams qualify for subsequent rounds based on the criteria announced for each round. Official answer sheets, timestamps, completion records, and verified results will be used for scoring and qualification. The decision of the authorised judges regarding scoring, qualification, and disqualification will be final.
09
CLUES, MOVEMENT & SAFETY
Teams must handle all clues, envelopes, data slips, props, and physical game elements carefully and follow designated movement routes and game zones. Entering restricted areas, damaging campus property, tampering with hidden elements, or unsafe movement is prohibited. The organising committee may modify routes, locations, or procedures when required for safety or operational reasons.
10
PRIZE DISTRIBUTION
Prize distribution will depend on the total number of registered teams:
  • Fewer than 15 teams: First Prize only
  • 15–30 teams: First & Second Prizes
  • More than 30 teams: First, Second & Third Prizes
11
MOVEMENT & SAFETY PROTOCOLS
Participants must remain within designated event areas and follow all instructions and safety protocols. Entering restricted areas, interfering with event arrangements, damaging campus property, or unsafe conduct is prohibited. The organising committee may modify locations or procedures when required for safety or operational reasons.

ℹ️ The organising committee may make necessary changes to the schedule, rules, or competition procedure due to technical or logistical circumstances.
