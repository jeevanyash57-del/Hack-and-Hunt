// about

What is Hack and Hunt?

A premier inter-college coding competition under VIGYANTRA at SJBIT, Bengaluru. One day. Real problems. One champion.

💡

What You'll Build

Teams design and develop working solutions to real-world problem statements revealed on competition day. Creativity and speed are both rewarded.

🎯

What You'll Learn

Rapid prototyping, team collaboration, time management under pressure, and pitching ideas to an expert panel — real skills, real exposure.

🤝

Teamwork First

Hack and Hunt is built on collaboration. From developer to presenter — every role matters. The best teams win together, not alone.

⚡

High-Intensity Format

Time-boxed rounds, live judging, and surprise constraints. Only the most adaptable and well-prepared teams rise to the top.

🌐

Open to All Domains

Web dev, AI/ML, cybersecurity, hardware, or business strategy — problem statements span multiple tracks so every talent fits.

🎖️

Recognition & Rewards

Cash prizes, trophies, and certificates for winners. Every participant receives a participation certificate from SJBIT / VIGYANTRA.

General Rules

Fair play is non-negotiable. Every team competes under identical conditions.

01
Team Eligibility & Size
Teams must consist of 2 to 4 members, all currently enrolled students with valid institutional IDs. Solo registrations are not permitted.
02
Code & Project Originality
All work must be original, created during the competition. Pre-built templates or previously developed code are prohibited. Plagiarism leads to disqualification.
03
Submission Deadline
All projects must be submitted by the announced deadline on competition day. Late submissions will not be accepted. Test and submit early.
04
Judging Criteria
Innovation (30%) · Technical Complexity (25%) · Practical Feasibility (25%) · Presentation (20%). Judges' decisions are final and binding.
05
Code of Conduct
Respect all participants, mentors, and judges. Any harassment, cheating, or misconduct results in immediate disqualification. Sportsmanship is mandatory.
06
Organizer Rights
VIGYANTRA organizers reserve the right to modify rules, timelines, or prize structures with prior notice. Registration implies agreement to all competition guidelines.
