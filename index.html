About

What is [EVENT NAME]?

A premier inter-college competition designed to challenge your technical skills, creativity, and ability to work under pressure.

💡

What You'll Build

Teams will design, develop, and present working solutions to real-world problem statements provided on competition day. Think creatively — originality is rewarded.

🎯

What You'll Learn

Beyond competing, participants gain hands-on exposure to rapid prototyping, team collaboration, time management, and pitching ideas to an expert panel.

🤝

Teamwork First

[EVENT NAME] is built on the belief that the best ideas come from diverse, collaborative teams. Every role — from developer to presenter — counts.

⚡

High-Intensity Format

Time-boxed rounds, live judging, and surprise constraints make this a true test of adaptability. Only the most prepared and agile teams rise to the top.

🌐

Open to All Domains

Whether you're into web dev, AI, hardware, design, or business strategy — there's a place for you. Problem statements span multiple domains.

Rules

General Rules

Fair play is non-negotiable. Every team competes under the same conditions.

01
Team Eligibility & Size
Teams must consist of 2 to 4 members. All members must be currently enrolled students with valid institutional ID cards. Solo registrations are not permitted.
02
Code & Project Originality
All submitted work must be original and created during the competition. Use of pre-built templates or previously developed code is strictly prohibited. Plagiarism leads to disqualification.
03
Submission Deadline
All projects must be submitted by [SUBMISSION DEADLINE]. Late submissions will not be accepted under any circumstances. Ensure you test and submit early to avoid last-minute issues.
04
Judging Criteria
Projects are evaluated on: Innovation & Creativity (30%), Technical Complexity (25%), Practical Feasibility (25%), Presentation & Communication (20%). Judges' decisions are final.
05
Code of Conduct
Respect all participants, mentors, and judges. Any form of harassment, cheating, or misconduct will result in immediate disqualification. Maintain sportsmanship at all times.
06
Organizer Rights
Organizers reserve the right to modify rules, timelines, or prize structures with prior notice. By registering, teams agree to abide by all competition guidelines as communicated.
