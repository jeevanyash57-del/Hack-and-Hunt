THE ARENA

WHAT IS HACK & HUNT?

The premier inter-college coding competition under VIGYANTRA '26 at SJBIT, Bengaluru. One day. Real problems. One champion squad.

💡

What You'll Build

Teams design and develop working solutions to real-world problem statements revealed on competition day. Creativity and speed are both rewarded.

🎯

What You'll Learn

Rapid prototyping, team collaboration, time management under pressure, and pitching ideas to an expert panel — real skills, real exposure.

🤝

Teamwork First

Hack and Hunt is built on collaboration. From developer to presenter — every role matters. The best teams win together, not alone.

⚡

High-Intensity Format

Time-boxed rounds, live judging, and surprise constraints across 04 intense rounds. Only the most adaptable teams rise to the top.

🌐

Open to All Domains

Web dev, AI/ML, cybersecurity, or algorithm design — problem statements span multiple tracks so every talent fits.

🎖️

Recognition & Rewards

Cash prizes, trophies, and certificates for winners. Every participant receives an official participation certificate from SJBIT / VIGYANTRA.

THE DIRECTIVES

OFFICIAL
RULES.

Master the parameters to conquer the arena. Read carefully before entering.

01
TEAM COMPOSITION
Each team must consist of exactly 4 registered members. Team members must remain unchanged throughout the event. Substitution or replacement is not permitted unless approved by the organising committee.
02
TEAM ID & IDENTIFICATION
Each team will receive a unique Team ID at registration. The assigned Team ID must be used throughout the event for identification, submissions, and official records.
03
REGISTERED EMAIL ID
The email ID provided during registration must be used throughout the event whenever an email ID is required, including submissions, forms, communication, and event-related activities.
04
REPORTING & CONDUCT
Teams must report at the scheduled time for every round and follow the instructions of organisers, volunteers, Station Masters, and judges. Professional and respectful conduct is expected.
05
PERMITTED RESOURCES
Only materials, devices, websites, and software specifically permitted for each round may be used. Mobile phones and unauthorised electronic resources are prohibited wherever specified.
06
FAIR PLAY
Sharing answers, clues, or solutions with other teams, receiving unauthorised assistance, observing another team's progress, or tampering with event materials is strictly prohibited. Violation leads to disqualification.
07
TIME LIMITS & SUBMISSIONS
Each round has a fixed time limit. All submissions must be completed within the specified time. Late submissions will not be considered. Official submission timestamps are used for scoring.
08
ROUND PROGRESSION & SCORING
Hack & Hunt follows a sequential elimination format. Teams qualify for subsequent rounds based on official criteria. The decision of the authorised judges will be final.
09
CLUES, MOVEMENT & SAFETY
Teams must handle all clues, data slips, props, and physical elements carefully and follow designated movement routes. Entering restricted areas or damaging campus property is prohibited.
10
PRIZE DISTRIBUTION TIERS
Prize distribution depends on total registered teams: • < 15 teams: First Prize only • 15–30 teams: First & Second Prizes • 30+ teams: First, Second & Third Prizes
11
SAFETY PROTOCOLS & NOTICE
Participants must remain within designated event areas and follow all safety protocols. The organising committee may modify schedule or procedures when required for technical or operational reasons.
